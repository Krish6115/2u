Make a birthday surprise

Fill this in once. You get a link to send. Everything below shows up in the 10 steps.

★ The basics

1 The letter

Shown inside the envelope. It opens with “Dear (name),” automatically. Blank line = new paragraph.

2 What I love about you (claw game)

Each line is one prize she grabs from the machine. 3 or more works best.

3 Our story (book)

One box per chapter. Chapter I, II, III… are numbered for you.

4 Wishes (wish jar)

Each wish appears when she taps “Make a wish”.

5 Photo booth

Up to 4 photos. They’re shrunk to fit inside the link. Add a short caption if you like.

6 Our places

Just names, in the order you went. No map needed.

7 A date to keep

8 Our song

Steps 9 (blow the candles) and 10 (final wish) are automatic: your age and both names are used there.
